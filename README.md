Atliq Grands Dashboard
Overview
Atliq Grands, a prestigious five-star hotel chain across India, is facing challenges in maintaining its market share and revenue due to strategic moves by competitors and ineffective internal decision-making. To regain their competitive edge, Atliq Grands is leveraging Business and Data Intelligence by analyzing their historical data. This project involves creating an interactive Power BI dashboard that provides actionable insights to support management in strategic decision-making.

Problem Statement
Atliq Grands has been losing market share in the luxury/business hotel category, which has impacted their revenue. Their revenue management team has outsourced the analysis of their historical data to deliver insights that will help regain their market share. This project focuses on delivering those insights using a comprehensive dashboard built in Power BI.

Objective
The objective of the Power BI dashboard is to:

Analyze historical data on hotel occupancy, revenue, customer preferences, and market trends.
Provide visual insights that enable decision-makers to:
Identify key areas of revenue loss.
Explore trends in customer preferences and demographics.
Understand the performance of different hotel locations across India.
Benchmark performance against competitors.
Key Insights Provided by the Dashboard
Revenue Analysis: Shows a breakdown of total revenue by hotel location, room type, and customer segments. It highlights top-performing properties and those with declining revenue.

Occupancy Trends: Displays trends in occupancy rates over time and provides a year-over-year comparison to identify peak seasons and underperforming months.

Customer Demographics & Preferences: Includes a visual breakdown of customer profiles, such as age, gender, and booking preferences (business vs. leisure), helping target specific customer groups for marketing.

Competitor Performance: Benchmarks Atliq Grands against competitors in the luxury hotel category, showing comparative analysis on room pricing, market share, and customer satisfaction.

Forecasting and Predictions: Utilizes historical data to provide revenue forecasting based on past trends, aiding in better budgeting and resource allocation for the coming quarters.

Power BI Dashboard Features
Interactive Visuals: The dashboard is fully interactive, enabling users to filter data by different dimensions (e.g., location, time period, customer segment) to gain granular insights.

Real-time Data Integration: While this dashboard is based on historical data, it can be integrated with real-time data sources for continuous monitoring.

User-Friendly Interface: Designed with the end user in mind, the dashboard provides a simple and intuitive interface for management to explore insights without requiring technical expertise.

Dashboard Overview
The Power BI dashboard consists of the following key sections:

Revenue Breakdown:

Total revenue by location
Revenue by room type and service offering
Trends over time with dynamic filters (location, time period, room type)
Occupancy Analysis:

Monthly and yearly occupancy rate trends
Occupancy comparison by hotel and region
Room type occupancy rates
Customer Insights:

Customer demographics breakdown (age, gender, booking type)
Customer preferences by location
Insights on repeat vs. new customers
Competitor Benchmarking:

Market share analysis against key competitors
Room pricing comparison
Comparative occupancy rates and customer satisfaction scores
Revenue Forecasting:

Predictive analytics for future revenue trends based on historical data
Visualization of forecast accuracy and confidence intervals

Expected Outcomes:
Regain their market share in business hotels category.
Understand the revenue trend by month/week/day.
Find where business is lagging and take important decisions to solve them.
